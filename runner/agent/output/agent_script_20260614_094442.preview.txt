 11. click          div:nth-of-type(1) > stock-indent-button > div > div:nth-of-type(2) > button:nth-of-type(2)   # Claim processes and Approve button appears
 12. click          div:nth-of-type(1) > stock-indent-button > div > div:nth-of-type(2) > button:nth-of-type(2)   # Claim is processed and Approve button appears
